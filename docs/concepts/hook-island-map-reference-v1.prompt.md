# 钩索小岛纯地图建模参考

生成方式：内置 image_gen。

参考原图：hook-island-low-poly-v1.png。

用途：Blender 地形、商店和码头建模的视觉参考；概念图不代表精确的工程投影或已验证的玩法尺寸。

## 最终提示词

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: a clean LOW-POLY island MAP reference sheet for later reconstruction in Blender. Generate ONE landscape image containing two consistent views of ONE identical environment.

Input image 1 is the design reference for the island, its warm sand, grass, grey faceted rocks, orange-awning upgrade shop and small wooden pier. Extract and simplify this environment into an unoccupied map. Preserve its compact, friendly visual identity.

Composition: two well-separated views on a plain very light warm-grey background, with generous margins and no border or text. On the LEFT show a true orthographic TOP-DOWN view looking straight down, which clearly reveals the whole shoreline footprint and the layout of the map. On the RIGHT show a somewhat larger orthographic three-quarter ISOMETRIC view of the SAME island, showing the land thickness, shop structure and dock supports. The two views must match in island outline, asset count, relative placements, terrain contours and materials. The entire island and pier must be completely inside the frame in both views. Do not let the views overlap.

Map layout: a very small irregular oval island, mostly flat and playable. A broad uncluttered green grassy clearing occupies the centre. A continuous pale sandy shore surrounds it. The orange canvas shop is at the north edge, facing south into the clearing; a short simple wooden plank pier projects southwest from the southwest shore. There is only ONE shop and ONE pier per view. Sparse low angular rock clusters sit at the northwest, northeast and southern perimeter, with broad unobstructed sandy gaps at west, south and east for players to approach the water. Make the shore gently slope down at these gaps. Keep the central walking area and routes to the shop, pier and shore completely clear. Add only a few very small clumps of faceted greenery beside the rear rocks. The unoccupied shop consists of four simple wooden posts, orange canopy, wooden counter and one barrel beside it; no stock clutter. The pier is a simple short rectangle of broad wooden planks supported by four posts, with no railings. The island has a shallow sand-and-rock foundation with visible low-polygon side faces: a low coastal island, not a tall floating mountain. Show the whole ground mesh as a clean isolated asset.

Materials and rendering: visibly flat-shaded low-polygon geometry, large readable polygon faces, simple matte solid-colour materials, minimal texture detail. Pale warm sand, muted grassy green, neutral grey stone, medium brown wood, saturated warm orange canopy. Soft neutral studio lighting with restrained contact shadows. Clear geometry and structural readability for a Blender modeller. The top view should have minimal shadows and no perspective distortion; the isometric view should clearly reveal heights and construction.

Remove ALL characters, merchant NPCs, fish, creatures, monsters, birds, coins, treasure chests, fishing equipment and active ropes from the original. No ocean surface, no waves, no foam, no underwater scenery, no detached offshore rocks, no distant background, no sky. The background is plain and opaque so the exact mesh silhouette is readable. No text, labels, dimension arrows, grids, interface, title, watermark or decorative frame. No additional buildings or props. This is strictly an EMPTY ENVIRONMENT modelling reference, not a gameplay action scene or promotional illustration.
